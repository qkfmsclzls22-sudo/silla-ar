# Gallery order and costume materials — 2026-10-22

The primary gallery order is now crown, Buddha smile, pensive Bodhisattva, gold wing ornament, earrings, smiling roof tile, king, queen, noblewoman, Hwarang. The roof tile is sixth, immediately before the four outfits.

## Images and materials

- Crown and roof-tile cards previously used dark-background photographs. They now use transparent renders of their existing 3D assets, with a common warm neutral studio background for all ten cards. The same renders are used in the shared photo camera.
- Costume v2 used flat colours and separate facing geometry. V3 adds baked woven colour, roughness and normal textures, retains the source garment folds and male robe pattern, and uses quieter metallic jewellery.
- Brocade panels and belts are baked directly onto garment UV surfaces. Dense embroidery curves and smaller jewellery follow the actual garment surface, removing the previous front-plane clamp that detached ornaments from the clothes.
- Crown bands are positioned by their wearing height rather than by the bottom of hanging pendants. The 3D-only faceless display form supports headwear; noble hairpins and the Hwarang band fit its head. The display form is absent from all photo overlays.
- Browser QA found excessive glTF sheen. The exported cloth sheen and normal intensity were reduced after actual WebGL inspection. Large mesh parts are simplified for mobile rendering; high-resolution photo renders retain their source geometry.
- The 3D viewer's adaptive render scale has a 0.75 floor so fabric details do not collapse to a blurry preview on slower devices. This uses the documented [model-viewer minimumRenderScale property](https://modelviewer.dev/docs/#entrydocs-loading-staticProperties-minimumRenderScale).

The costumes remain creative, drama-inspired experiences based on official Korea Heritage Service robe/top/skirt data. They are not scans of complete royal outfits or authenticated reconstructions. Existing source links and original assets are retained.

- Queen photo import, capture and save were exercised using a non-personal illustrated fitting mannequin. The 900 × 1200 PNG was downloaded and verified on disk. Actual user faces are not stored or sent to a server.

## Limits

Desktop mobile-width checks do not verify a physical phone camera, the native share sheet, ARCore or iOS Quick Look. Photo positioning remains manual, with separate clothing/headwear controls. Fabric maps add detail but do not make the creative costumes photographic scans.
